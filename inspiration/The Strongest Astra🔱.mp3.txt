Brahmastra is the weakest weapon when compared to these three terrifying weapons.
First, Pashupatastra.
When Shivji gave it to Arjun,
he warned him that using it against a weaker enemy could burn the entire universe.
And Arjun didn't even need to draw his bow.
He could unleash it through his words, his eyes, or just his mind and withdraw it afterward.
Anushasan Parva places it above the Brahma and Narayan weapons.
Second, Sudarshan Chakra.
Ashwathama possessed Brahmashirastra, yet he wanted Krishna's chakra.
Krishna let him try to take it.
Ashwathama used all his strength but couldn't even lift it.
The Bhagavad Purana describes the chakra as a destroyer of weapons.
And finally, Shivji's Trishul.
The Mahabharata compares it with Pashupatastra itself.
In Mahadev's hands, it could split the earth, dry the oceans, and destroy the entire universe.
