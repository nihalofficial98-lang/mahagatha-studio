Kumbhkaran struck Hanumanji so hard that he vomited blood.
During the battle in Lanka, Hanumanji attacked Kumbhkaran with enormous mountain peaks.
But Kumbhkaran fought back, driving his spear into Hanumanji's chest.
Dawalmi Ki Ramayana, Yudhakaand, Sarga 67, verse 20 says,
Sir Shula Nirbhinna Mahabhujan Tarah Pravehala Shonita Mudvaman Mukhat
Hanumanji was severely wounded with blood pouring from his mouth, but he wasn't defeated.
When Kumbhkaran hurled his spear at Sugriva, Hanumanji leapt forward,
got the weapon and snapped it across his knee.
Later, when Kumbhkaran captured Sugriva, Hanumanji considered enlarging himself to the size of a mountain
and killing him with his fists.
But he trusted Sugriva to escape on his own and Sugriva did exactly that.
Kumbhkaran continued fighting until Sriram confronted him.
Sriram severed his arms, cut off his feet and finally beheaded him with a divine arrow.
