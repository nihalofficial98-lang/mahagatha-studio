Mahadev once killed Yamaraj just to protect a 16-year-old bhakt.
His name was Markandeya, and his father, Rishi Mrikandu, had received a boon from Mahadev,
a wise and virtuous son who would live for only 16 years.
But Markandeya grew up to be a devoted worshipper of Mahadev,
and when he turned 16, Yamaraj himself came to take his life.
Terrified, Markandeya embraced the Shivling and began praying.
But Yamaraj threw his noose around Markandeya, and it caught the Shivling too.
This made Mahadev so furious that he emerged from the Shivling and killed Yamaraj.
But without Yamaraj, who would govern death?
So the devas pleaded with Mahadev, who revived Yamaraj and blessed Markandeya with eternal youth.
