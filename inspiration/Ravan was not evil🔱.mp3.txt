Ravan never touched Maasita. Ravan wasn't evil. Well, not really.
When Hanumanji entered Ravan's palace, he found chambers filled with women.
Sundarkand, Sarg-9, Shloka-69 mentions,
Yuddha Kameena Taha, Sarva Ravanena Hrithaha Striyaha.
Meaning, women had been taken by Ravan during his conquests.
But Ravan himself confessed something darker.
He had sexually assaulted an Apsara named Punjikasthala.
Stripping her and forcing himself on her.
When Brahma discovered this, he cursed Ravan.
If he ever sexually violated another woman, his head would split into 100 pieces.
And now about the claim that he never touched Maasita.
Aranya Kand, Sarg-49, Shloka-17 says,
Ravan grabbed Maasita's hair with one hand and her thighs with the other while abducting her.
And in Yuddha Kand, Ravan himself admits he couldn't force Maasita into his bed
because he feared Brahma's curse.
Not because he respected her.
Because he feared the curse!
