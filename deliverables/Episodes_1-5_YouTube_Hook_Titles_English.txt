FACT-CHECK NOTES (what I changed after checking every script)
======================================================
EP1: "Vishnu fought 5,000 years and lost" was wrong: the script says he began to tire, not lose. Fixed.
EP2: Removed "Why almost nobody worships Brahma" (not in the script) and "Why did Rudra cry" (he asks his name; he does not cry).
EP3: Removed "Shiva cut himself in half": the script says his body changed, half man and half woman.
EP4: All claims checked (5 heads, skull/Kapali, 100 forms, Kashi, Maya glass of water).
EP5: Removed "Sati's father did not listen" (not in the script) and "Real reason Vishnu had to kill Ravana" (reworded to the boon, which is in the script). Kumaras are written as "child-sages", since they look like children but are ancient.
EP5 caution: "Ravana" and "Shishupala" appear only as the later births (the script names them once, in the three-lives list).
