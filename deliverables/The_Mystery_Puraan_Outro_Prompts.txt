THE MYSTERY PURAAN: CHANNEL OUTRO PROMPTS
Length: 15 seconds (YouTube end screens work best at 15 to 20 s). 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps.
Use in Veo / Runway / Kling / Sora / Luma / Pika. Text is better added afterwards in an editor (see "TEXT OVERLAYS"), because AI video tools often misspell text.

====================================================
1. MAIN PROMPT (paste this)
====================================================
A cinematic 15-second animated channel outro for a mythology storytelling channel called "The Mystery Puraan". High-quality anime / graphic-novel illustration style, rich dark indigo, deep crimson and glowing gold colours, heroic adult proportions, thick ink outlines, dramatic rim light.

Shot 1 (0 to 3 s): Black screen. A single tiny golden flame lights up in the darkness. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal an ancient palm-leaf manuscript (puraan) lying open on a stone altar, its ink glowing faintly.

Shot 2 (3 to 7 s): The glowing letters rise off the manuscript like fireflies and swirl up into the sky, forming a spinning galaxy. Inside the galaxy the silhouettes of a four-faced Brahma, Vishnu on a serpent bed, and Shiva with a trident appear for a moment, drawn in gold light, then dissolve into sparks.

Shot 3 (7 to 10 s): The camera rushes through the stars and clouds toward a huge glowing golden lotus. The lotus opens; in its centre, the channel logo emblem appears: a mysterious eye with a lotus petal pattern inside a ring of fire, with a crescent moon on top. A shockwave of golden light rolls outward.

Shot 4 (10 to 15 s): The camera settles on a calm end-screen scene: a dark temple courtyard with floating diyas and drifting petals. The empty space in the centre and right stays calm and dark, to hold two video thumbnails. At the bottom left, a glowing red SUBSCRIBE button appears and a golden bell swings and rings. Embers float up gently. A subtle gold shimmer sweeps across the logo at the end.

Mood: mysterious, epic, curious. Smooth cinematic camera moves, parallax depth, floating embers and petals, no sudden flashing. No real-person faces, no watermark.

====================================================
6. END-SCREEN LAYOUT (YouTube end screen)
====================================================
- Two video slots: one on the right-centre (latest video), one below it (best-performing video), each about 33% of the frame width.
- Round subscribe icon at the bottom left, with the bell next to it.
- Keep these areas calm and dark in the animation so the elements do not clash with the picture.

====================================================
7. QUICK FIXES
====================================================
- Logo looks wrong: describe it simply ("a glowing eye in a lotus ring with a crescent moon") and add your own logo in the editor.
- Too fast or flashy: add "slow cinematic camera, no strobing flashes".
- Style is off: add "premium anime illustration, hand-painted background, rich lighting".
- AI text is misspelt: remove all text from the prompt and add it in the editor.
