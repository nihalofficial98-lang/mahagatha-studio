SERIES RULES (so the channel looks like one brand, but never boring)
- Always: ONE emotional face (a huge close-up or a strong silhouette), ONE strong colour idea, ONE curiosity object (a glowing hand, a head, a gate, a trident).
- Different palette, composition and text design every episode. Same font family, same small "EP #" badge, same bold outline.
- Every thumbnail has a "what is that?" detail the viewer wants to understand.
- The face is at the right or centre; the text sits on the empty area; never more than 4 words.

QUICK FIXES
=====================================================
- Faces too soft: "mature realistic anime, intense, sharp jawline".
- Colours dull: "high saturation, deep contrast, glowing highlights".
- Wrong count of arms/heads: say "exactly four arms" / "exactly four faces" and re-generate.
- Too busy: "simple clear composition, one hero object".
- Shiva: "a coiled round bun of matted hair on top with a crescent moon, no long flowing hair".
