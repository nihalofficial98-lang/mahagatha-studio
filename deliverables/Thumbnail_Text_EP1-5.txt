HOW I WOULD STYLE THEM (works with our coded characters)
======================================================
- Font: heavy Devanagari or Latin display (Impact / Anton style), cream or yellow fill with a thick dark outline and a soft shadow.
- Colour: one highlight word in red (anger) or gold (divine). Everything else white or cream.
- Layout: text on one side (about one third of the frame), a big close-up character face on the other.
- Background: the dark red or midnight-blue paper style with glowing rays. Characters are drawn large with strong rim light.
- Keep a tiny episode number badge ("EP 4") in the top corner so the series reads as one channel.
