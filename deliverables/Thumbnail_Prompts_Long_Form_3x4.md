# Thumbnails for the long videos EP01 to EP07 (3:4, 1080x1440)
Use these as the cover for Instagram and Facebook. 3:4 is the size the Instagram grid shows, so nothing gets cropped. Titles are taken from library/EPISODE_PLAN.md (EP06 and EP07 from the recorded episodes). Please check that each title matches the video you posted, and tell me if one is wrong.

## Layout rules (percent of the 1080x1440 image, from the top)
- Everything important stays at least 6% away from every edge.
- 6 to 34%: TITLE, centred, two short lines, first line white and the key word bright yellow, thick black outline.
- 32 to 88%: the main character's face and upper body, large, centred, strong clear emotion.
- A small "EP 01" style badge in the top-left corner is fine, but keep it small and outside the title.

## Master prompt (paste first, then one episode block)
Vertical 3:4 cover image, 1080x1440, for a long Indian mythology video. Bold, vivid, painterly illustration like a premium comic cover: rich saturated colours, dramatic glowing light behind the hero, thick clean outlines, high contrast, readable at the size of a small phone thumbnail. A huge bold title sits in the top third, centred, on two short lines in a clean, simple, very readable bold sans-serif font (like Montserrat ExtraBold, solid straight letters, all capitals), first line plain white and the key word solid yellow, a thin dark outline and a soft plain shadow, no neon, no glow, no brush or handwritten style, no decoration, letters filling about 80% of the width without touching the edges. The main character's face and upper body are large and fully visible in the middle, centred, with a strong clear emotion. Keep 6% of empty margin around every edge. No watermark, not photorealistic, not a 3D render, no extra text except the title. Spell the title exactly as given.

## Episode blocks

**EP01. Title lines: "BIRTH OF" / "CREATION"**  (key word: CREATION)  (subtitle badge: "+ Madhu and Kaitabha")
Scene: Vishnu sleeping on the cosmic ocean on the serpent Shesha, a golden lotus rising from his navel with Brahma on it. At the bottom corners, two fierce demon faces (Madhu and Kaitabha) rise from the water, looking up. Palette: deep blue ocean, gold lotus.

**EP02. Title lines: "SHIVA" / "APPEARS"**  (key word: APPEARS)
Scene: Shiva's huge glowing face and upper body, third eye just opening, rising behind a small angry Brahma and a small calm Narada who face each other in the foreground. Palette: fiery orange behind Shiva, deep night blue.

**EP03. Title lines: "WHO MADE" / "HUMANS?"**  (key word: HUMANS)
Scene: Shiva as Ardhanarishwara (right half male, left half female), calm face, golden light, a small Brahma looking up in awe at the bottom. Palette: gold and blue cosmic sky.

**EP04. Title lines: "SHIVA CUT" / "BRAHMA'S HEAD"**  (key word: HEAD)
Scene: Shiva, fierce and tired, a skull stuck to his hand, with four-faced Brahma behind him now showing only four faces and one empty gap, with a bright river ghat at sunrise far behind. Palette: orange sunrise, ash blue. Keep it clean, no gore.

**EP05. Title lines: "THE CURSE OF" / "JAYA AND VIJAYA"**  (key word: CURSE)
Scene: two gatekeepers in golden armour at the gate of Vaikuntha, faces shocked and afraid, while four glowing child sages block the gate with raised hands and angry eyes. Vishnu's calm blue face glows far behind them. Palette: gold gates, blue sky, red flash of the curse.

**EP06. Title lines: "WHY NO ONE" / "WORSHIPS BRAHMA"**  (key word: BRAHMA)
Scene: Brahma with four faces, looking guilty, holding a white ketaki flower, in front of a giant glowing pillar of fire; Shiva's fierce glow inside the pillar. Palette: fire orange, gold, deep night blue.

**EP07. Title lines: "KACHA STEALS" / "A DEAD SECRET"**  (key word: SECRET)
Scene: young sage Kacha, face cold and half-smiling, standing in front of the old guru Shukracharya who sits behind him glowing red; a girl, Devayani, in the corner looks at him with hope. Dark, moody red, orange and brown palette with deep shadow. Mood: betrayal.

## Check before you post
Shrink the image to a small square on your phone. The title and the face should both be easy to read. If not, ask the tool to make the letters bigger and move the face to the centre.

## Font options to try (all simple and readable)
1. Montserrat ExtraBold (default in the prompt).
2. Bebas Neue style: tall, narrow, capitals. Fits more letters on a line.
3. Anton or Impact style: very heavy and narrow, strongest at a small size.
To test one, replace the font name in the master prompt. If the tool spells the text wrongly, generate the picture without text and add the title in Canva with the same font.
