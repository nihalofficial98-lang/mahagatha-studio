REEL 17: The God Who Tore Out His Eye (How Did Vishnu Get the Sudarshana Chakra?)
[curious] Vishnu, keeper of the universe, tore out his own eye. [pause] He did it for Shiva. And Shiva answered with the one weapon in the universe that flies back to its master, the Sudarshana Chakra. [pause]
[grave] It began with demons. They tormented the gods and killed people. The gods begged Vishnu to save them. Vishnu knew he needed Shiva's power. [calm] So he went into the wilderness and worshipped Shiva. Years passed. Shiva did not appear. [pause]
[calm] So Vishnu shaped a lingam, the sacred form of Shiva, out of earth. He chanted the thousand names of Shiva. For every name, he laid one lotus on the lingam. [surprised] When the chant ended, a thousand lotuses covered it. Shiva was moved. But he wanted to test Vishnu one last time, so he took one lotus away. [pause]
[tense] Vishnu counted the flowers. Nine hundred and ninety-nine. He searched everywhere, but the lotus was gone. One flower short meant the worship was not complete. [dramatic] Would Vishnu fail in front of Shiva? [pause]
[firm] Then Vishnu smiled. One of his names was Kamalanayana, the one with lotus eyes. His eyes were like lotuses, so one of them could take the place of the flower. [shocked] He pulled out his own eye and placed it on the lingam. The thousandth lotus. [pause]
[warm] Shiva appeared at once and healed Vishnu's eye. Then he gave him a golden disc with two circles spinning in opposite directions. [confident] He named it Sudarshana, which means virtuous vision. That is how Vishnu got the Sudarshana Chakra. Vishnu gave his eye, and Shiva gave him the weapon. [pause]

REEL 8: When Vishnu's Head Was Cut Off
[shocked] The gods cut off Vishnu's head. They did it on purpose. Every one of them had turned into a termite. [pause] How did Vishnu lose his head? [pause]
[calm] Vishnu protects the world. He had fought one demon for sixteen thousand years and could not win. His mace flew back and smashed his own chariot. The demon told him that only someone who knew his secret could kill him. [pause]
[calm] Vishnu went home, very tired, and fell asleep with his chin resting on his bow. [serious] The gods, led by Brahma, who made the world, needed Vishnu awake for a fire prayer called a yagna. Their own powers had failed against the demon. Brahma had an idea. If the bowstring snapped, the sound would shake the whole sky. [pause]
[firm] So the gods turned into termites and chewed the string. Thinner and thinner. Faster and faster. [dramatic] Then it snapped, and the string whipped loose and cut off Vishnu's head. It rolled away. His eyes were still closed. [shocked] The gods froze. They knew they had made a terrible mistake. [pause]
[curious] Then Lakshmi, his wife, walked in. Her feet left red marks on the floor. She asked them to forgive her. [serious] The demon had prayed to her for so long that she had given him a gift. He could only be killed by a god with a horse's head. [pause]
[surprised] That was why the demon could not be beaten. Lakshmi told the gods to bring the head of a horse and put it on Vishnu. He became Hayagriva, the horse-headed god. [confident] Soon Vishnu neighed, and the demon fell silent. [pause]
[calm] The gods cut off Vishnu's head by mistake, only to wake him. And that mistake gave them the one god who could kill the demon. [pause]

REEL 16: The Demon Who Agreed to Die (Why Did Bala Agree to Die?)
[mysterious] Bala was a demon so strong that he beat Indra and took heaven. [dramatic] No god could kill him. But he agreed to die. No one forced him. The gods only asked. [pause] Why did Bala agree to die? [pause]
[calm] Bala was a demon king, but his heart was kind. He had never refused anyone who asked him for a gift. The gods stood outside heaven with nowhere to go. [warm] Bala walked up to them and told them he had always given freely, and he would give again. Whenever they held a yagna, a fire ritual, he would help. Indra smiled. [pause]
[serious] The gods held their yagna. A yagna needs a being to offer to the fire. They bowed to Bala and asked him to offer himself. [shocked] The hall froze. Bala stood up and came closer. And closer. Then he folded his hands and said he was ready. [pause]
[grave] The flames took his body. Then someone cried out. The burned body was turning into something so bright that no one could look at it. The gods tried to lift it to heaven, but it slipped. It fell to earth and broke into eighty-four pieces. [pause]
[surprised] Wherever they landed, they became jewels. His bones became diamonds. His blood became rubies. His eyes became sapphires. His teeth became pearls. His voice, the voice that said he was ready, became the cat's eye. [pause]
[firm] Nine of those jewels are called the Navaratnas. That is why Bala agreed to die. He could never say no to anyone who asked. The gods won heaven by a trick. And the earth won a treasure. [pause]
