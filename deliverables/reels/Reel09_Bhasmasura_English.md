## Plain narration
Why did Shiva, the Destroyer of worlds, run for his life from a demon he had blessed himself? The demon's gift could turn anyone to ash, and only Vishnu could save Shiva. Vishnu needed a trick to do it.
The demon was Vrikasura. The sage Narada told him to win a boon, a gift from a god. He prayed for hundreds of years. Shiva did not come. So he started cutting off his own flesh. As his sword reached his neck, Shiva caught his arm.
"Make me magical," said the demon. "Anyone I touch on the head turns to ash." "But that is cruel," said Shiva. "If you give it, it proves you love me." Shiva agreed. He named him Bhasmasura, the ash demon.
Then Parvati, Shiva's wife, walked down the hill. The demon stared at her. He wanted her, and her husband was in the way. He raised his hand. Shiva stepped back. He ran.
He ran in all ten directions. Every time he looked back, the demon was there. Only one place was left: Vaikuntha, the home of Vishnu.
"Protect me," said Shiva. Vishnu vanished. A beautiful woman stood in his place. Mohini. The demon forgot his gift. "Come," she said. "Dance with me."
Then she put her hand on her head. So did he.
Mohini laughed and spun around a pile of ash. Shiva ran because his own gift would not spare even him. Vishnu saved him by making the demon dance into it.
