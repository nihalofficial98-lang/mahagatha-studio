## Plain narration
A demon asked Shiva for a boon that could turn anyone to ash. Shiva said yes. Then the demon looked at Shiva's head.
The demon was Vrikasura, a devotee of Shiva, the Destroyer. Narada, the trouble-maker sage, told him he was no true devotee without a boon. So he prayed on Mount Kailash for hundreds of years. Shiva did not come. He began cutting off parts of his body as offerings. As his sword reached his neck, Shiva caught his arm and healed him.
Vrikasura asked that anyone he touched on the head turn to ashes. Shiva called it violent. Vrikasura said it would prove Shiva's love. Shiva agreed and named him Bhasmasura, the ash demon.
Then Parvati, Shiva's wife, came down the slope. Bhasmasura stared. He could not have her while her husband lived. He raised his hand toward Shiva's head.
Shiva ran. The boon would not spare even him. He ran in all ten directions, but the demon was always right behind. At last he reached Vaikuntha, the home of Vishnu.
Vishnu smiled. He already knew. Shiva asked for protection. Vishnu vanished, and a woman stood in his place.
She was Mohini. Bhasmasura forgot everything, even his boon. She said, "Come. Dance with me." He copied every step and every turn.
Then she placed her hand on her head. So did he.
Mohini laughed as she whirled around a pile of ash.
