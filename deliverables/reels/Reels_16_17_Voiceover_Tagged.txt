REEL 16: Why Did Bala, the Demon Who Beat Indra, Agree to Die?
[mysterious] A demon so strong he beat Indra and took heaven. No god could touch him. But one thing could beat him, a request. Why did Bala agree to die?
[calm] Bala was a demon king, but his heart was kind. He had never refused anyone who asked him for a gift. The gods stood outside heaven with nowhere to go. Bala walked up to them. "I have given freely before, and I will give again," he said. "Whenever you hold a yagna, a fire ritual, I will help you." Indra smiled.
[fast] The gods held a yagna. A yagna needs a being to offer. They bowed to Bala and said, "We ask you to offer yourself." The hall froze. Bala stood up. He came closer. And closer. Then he folded his hands. "I am ready."
[grave] The flames took his body. Then someone screamed, "Look!" The burned body was turning into something so bright that no one could look away. The gods tried to lift it to heaven. It slipped. It fell to earth and broke into eighty-four pieces. Wherever they landed, they became jewels. His bones became diamonds. His blood became rubies. His eyes became sapphires. His teeth became pearls. His voice, the voice that said "I am ready," became the cat's eye.
[firm] Nine of those jewels are called the Navaratnas. That is why Bala agreed to die. He could never say no to anyone who asked. The gods won heaven by a trick. The earth won a treasure.

REEL 17: How Did Vishnu Get the Sudarshana Chakra?
[curious] To get the Sudarshana Chakra, Vishnu had to pull out his own eye. And he did it for another god. How did Vishnu get the Sudarshana Chakra?
[grave] It began with demons. They tormented the gods and killed people. The gods begged Vishnu to save them. Vishnu was tired of the endless attacks. He knew he needed Shiva's power. He went into the wilderness and worshipped Shiva. Years passed. Shiva did not appear.
[calm] So Vishnu shaped a lingam, the sacred form of Shiva, out of earth. He chanted the thousand names of Shiva. For every name, he laid one lotus on the lingam. When the chant ended, a thousand lotuses covered it. Shiva was moved. But he wanted to test Vishnu one last time. He quietly took one lotus.
[whispering] Vishnu counted the flowers. Nine hundred and ninety-nine. He searched everywhere. The lotus was gone. One flower short meant the worship was unfinished. Would Vishnu fail before Shiva?
[firm] Then Vishnu smiled. One of his names was Kamalanayana, the one with lotus eyes. "If my eyes are like lotuses," he said, "one of them can go on the lingam." He plucked out his eye and placed it there. The thousandth lotus.
[grand] Shiva appeared at once and healed Vishnu's eye. Then he gave him a golden disc with two circles spinning in opposite directions. "Call it Sudarshana," he said, "virtuous vision." That is how Vishnu got the Sudarshana Chakra. He gave an eye, and Shiva gave him the weapon.
