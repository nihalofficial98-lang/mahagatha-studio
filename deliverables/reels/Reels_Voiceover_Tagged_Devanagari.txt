=== Did Shiva Create the First Humans? | Did Shiva Create the First Humans? Brahma Could Not Do It Alone ===

[curious] Everyone says ब्रह्मा, the Creator, made the first humans.
[calm] But ब्रह्मा could not do it. His own sons had rejected him, and creation was wearing him down.
[curious] So who showed him how? The one god he believed could not create.
[calm] विष्णु, the Preserver, noticed ब्रह्मा's grief. He told him to go to महादेव, another name for शिव. ब्रह्मा said the Destroyer could not create. विष्णु said शिव might not create, but he would surely counsel.
ब्रह्मा found शिव meditating. He sat down and prayed from the depth of his soul. शिव opened his eyes. He said reproduction could not happen the way ब्रह्मा had made his sons. What ब्रह्मा needed was शक्ति, the power of the feminine.
Then शिव's body changed. The right side became male. The left side became female, split evenly down the middle. This form was called अर्धनारीश्वर, the Androgynous One.
[firm] शिव said masculine and feminine were the two halves of the cosmos. Neither could work without the other.
[calm] ब्रह्मा bowed to the Goddess and asked to create the female. She gave him the generative force. From his own body, ब्रह्मा made a man named मनु and a woman named ब्राह्मी. The human race descends from मनु.
[curious] The Destroyer had not made the first humans. He had shown the Creator what creation needed, both halves.

=== Why Do People Go to Kashi? | Why Do People Go to Kashi? It Started With Shiva and Brahma's Head ===

[curious] Why do people travel to काशी, also called वाराणसी, to be freed of their sins?
[calm] The answer begins with ब्रह्मा, the Creator, who once had five heads.
[curious] शिव cut one off in rage. Then it would not let go of him.
It began with a curse. The sage नारद had said ब्रह्मा would desire what must not be desired. When ब्रह्मा created his daughter ब्राह्मी, the curse took hold. She saw his gaze and moved away. Each time she moved, a new head grew so he could keep looking at her.
[firm] His sons मरीचि and अत्रि warned him. ब्राह्मी became a cow, a mare, a duck, a doe, a hundred forms in all. She was named शतरूपा.
शिव appeared in fury. ब्रह्मा came to his senses and begged forgiveness. शिव cut off the fifth head. ब्रह्मा's four mouths would recite the वेद forever, to repent.
[curious] Then शिव stopped. The severed head was stuck to his left hand.
[calm] Killing a ब्राह्मण was a sin. अत्रि said every action brings its result, whatever the reason. ब्रह्मा had paid, though a curse drove him. So must शिव.
शिव wandered the universe, but nothing freed him. The flesh rotted away until only a skull remained. He was called कपाली.
[firm] Then he reached a place on Earth, and the skull fell from his hand. Anyone who came there would be freed of every sin. That place became काशी, also called वाराणसी, and that is why people still go there.

=== Madhu and Kaitabha | How Did Vishnu Defeat Madhu and Kaitabha? ===

[curious] विष्णु, the Preserver, fought two brothers for five thousand years and could not win. Then he put down his mace and offered them a gift. But the gift was never for them.
[calm] The brothers were मधु and कैटभ. They crawled out of विष्णु's earwax when ब्रह्मा, the Creator, brought forth the वेद. They fed on the endless water and grew huge.
They found ब्रह्मा on his lotus and told him to bow or fight. He stayed silent, so they shook the stem. The वेद slipped from his hands, and the brothers hid them.
ब्रह्मा begged विष्णु, but विष्णु lay in योग निद्रा, a trance between waking and sleep. ब्रह्मा called on निद्रा to release him. विष्णु woke as हयग्रीव, the Horse-Headed One.
When one brother tired, the other took over. विष्णु saw strength would never win. So he laid down his mace, praised their power, and offered a boon.
[curious] The brothers sneered. Victors did not beg for boons, they said. They gave them. They told him to ask. विष्णु asked, "Grant me that I may kill you both."
[firm] They had walked into a trap, but they had given their word. They asked to die only where there was no water. विष्णु grew, towering over the waters like a blue mountain. He placed the brothers on his thighs and beheaded them.
[calm] For killing मधु, विष्णु came to be called मधुसूदन. ब्रह्मा asked him to keep saving them.
[curious] विष्णु said, "Perhaps I did nothing. Perhaps the universe saved itself through me."

=== Narada's Maya Test | Narada Asked Vishnu What Maya Is. Then This Happened ===

[curious] नारद, a great sage, asked विष्णु, the Preserver, what माया is. विष्णु did not explain. He asked for water. नारद never brought it, and that was the answer.
[calm] नारद had come to वैकुंठ, विष्णु's home, to tell him how his curse had come true on his father, ब्रह्मा. विष्णु smiled and said it was all माया. नारद asked what that meant.
विष्णु said he was thirsty. He told नारद to fetch some water, and then he would answer.
नारद walked to the river outside. By the bank he saw a young woman. She was so lovely that he forgot विष्णु, his errand and his thirst.
[curious] He married her right there and built a house on the sand by the river.
[calm] Years passed. नारद became a father, then a grandfather. The house had become his whole world.
Then one day the sky turned grey. Thunder roared. Rain poured, and the river flooded. The water swept his family away.
[firm] Suddenly नारद was back in वैकुंठ, standing before विष्णु. विष्णु asked, "Where is the water? I told you I am thirsty."
नारद stood stunned. His wife, his children, his home. None of it had been real.
विष्णु said that was माया. Attachment to a world that does not last. It makes us chase what slips away like sand.
[curious] Then he smiled and warned नारद that there would be much more माया to come.

=== Shiva Born From Anger | How Was Shiva Born From Brahma's Anger? ===

[curious] शिव was born from another god's anger. His first demand was not for power. It was for a name.
[calm] ब्रह्मा, the Creator, had made four sons from his mind. They were child sages called the कुमार. He told them to help him fill the world with living beings. They folded their hands and said no.
They said they were grateful, but they did not want to be bound by life and death. They wanted to meditate on the infinite. They asked only that they never grow old. ब्रह्मा argued, then gave in. As his sons left, rage rose in him.
[curious] A fiery beam burst from his forehead. A being stepped out of it with blazing red eyes. He roared, "What is my name?"
[calm] ब्रह्मा sat in awe. The being roared again. "Tell me my name." ब्रह्मा remembered that the root word for both red and roaring is रुद. He said, "रुद्र. I name you रुद्र."
This was शिव, also called महादेव. ब्रह्मा created, विष्णु preserved, and शिव was the Destroyer.
ब्रह्मा asked him to help with creation. शिव agreed and began making beings. But he made copies of himself, and each was immortal. Eleven रुद्र stood before ब्रह्मा.
[firm] ब्रह्मा said creation needed mortals, who die and are born again. शिव said he could only make immortals. If that spoiled the plan, he would rather meditate. Then he left.
[curious] The god born from ब्रह्मा's anger could only make what never dies.

=== Why Say Swaha? | Why Does Every Offering to Agni End With Swaha? ===

[curious] Every offering to the fire god अग्नि ends with the word स्वाहा. The gods once starved while humans fed the fire regularly. The fire god himself said it was his fault.
[calm] One morning, the gods came to ब्रह्मा, the Creator, in ब्रह्मलोक. They were thin and weak. They had nothing to eat.
ब्रह्मा explained how they were meant to eat. When humans held a यज्ञ, a fire ritual, they poured offerings into the fire. अग्नि, the mouth of the gods, carried them to heaven. In return, the gods gave humans blessings.
[curious] इंद्र, a leader of the gods, said humans held यज्ञ all the time. But nothing reached heaven.
[calm] Another god, कामदेव, blamed अग्नि. अग्नि stepped forward with folded hands. He said he was to blame. He could burn the offerings, but he could not carry them to the gods. He did not have that skill.
ब्रह्मा had no answer. विष्णु, the Preserver, told him to pray to प्रकृति, Mother Nature.
प्रकृति created a young goddess to carry offerings from Earth to heaven. ब्रह्मा asked her name.
[firm] "स्वाहा," she said.
ब्रह्मा declared that every offering to अग्नि must end with her name. Without it, the offering would not reach the gods. A chant without स्वाहा would be like a tree without fruit, a snake without venom, a man without knowledge.
[curious] Then he gave स्वाहा in marriage to अग्नि, and the two were never parted.

=== Daksha Cursed Narada | Why Did Daksha Curse Narada to Never Find a Home? ===

[curious] दक्ष, the son of ब्रह्मा, lost six thousand sons. Every single one. And the man who took them never raised a hand.
[calm] दक्ष had five thousand sons, called the हर्यश्व. He told them to rule the Earth and have children. They agreed. Before they set out, they gathered where the River सिंधु meets the sea to make offerings.
नारद, the divine sage, was on his way to them. [curious] He asked how they could rule a land they knew nothing about. How would they know if it had too few people or too many? The brothers had no answer. नारद smirked. He told them to learn every detail about the Earth first. They might even find a higher path.
Doubt crept over five thousand faces. The brothers left to explore the Earth. No one saw them again. They never came back.
दक्ष and his wife असिक्नी had another thousand sons, the शबलाश्व. नारद spoke to them in exactly the same way. They vanished too.
[firm] दक्ष roared. Who would build homes if everyone became a hermit? नारद said he had only shown them a path. It was their choice to walk it.
दक्ष cursed him. नारद had scattered his sons across the Earth, so he would wander the three worlds for ever and never find a home.
[curious] Was नारद a guide, or a thief of sons?

=== Vishnu Lost His Head | Vishnu Fought a Demon for 16,000 Years. Then He Lost His Head. ===

[curious] विष्णु, the Preserver, fought the demon हयग्रीव for sixteen thousand years. He still could not win, and then he lost his head. The ones who cut it off were the gods.
[calm] Nothing worked on the demon. विष्णु's mace was thrown back and smashed his chariot. His सुदर्शन disc stopped at an invisible wall. The demon laughed. Only one who knew his secret could kill him.
[calm] Worn out, विष्णु went home to वैकुंठ and fell into a deep sleep, his chin on his bow. ब्रह्मा, the Creator, and the gods came to wake him for a यज्ञ, a fire ritual. Nothing worked.
[calm] ब्रह्मा had an idea. विष्णु was sleeping on his bowstring. If it snapped, the sound would wake him. The gods turned into termites and gnawed at the string. Thinner and thinner. Faster and faster. Then it snapped.
[firm] The string lashed out and cut off विष्णु's head. Silence. The gods stood frozen. What have we done?
[curious] Then लक्ष्मी, विष्णु's wife, walked in, leaving red footprints. She asked the gods to forgive her. The demon was unbeatable because of a boon, and the boon was hers. His devotion had touched her. He could only be killed by a god with the head of a horse.
[calm] लक्ष्मी said the universe is never random. It looks after itself. विष्णु would take a horse's head and become the Horse-Headed One.
[calm] Not long after, the horse-headed विष्णु neighed aloud, and the demon हयग्रीव fell silent.
[curious] Was it a mistake, or the only way?

=== Shiva Ran From a Devotee | Shiva Ran From His Own Devotee ===

[curious] A demon asked शिव for a boon that could turn anyone to ash. शिव said yes. Then the demon looked at शिव's head.
[calm] The demon was वृकासुर, a devotee of शिव, the Destroyer. नारद, the trouble-maker sage, told him he was no true devotee without a boon. So he prayed on Mount कैलाश for hundreds of years. शिव did not come. He began cutting off parts of his body as offerings. As his sword reached his neck, शिव caught his arm and healed him.
[calm] वृकासुर asked that anyone he touched on the head turn to ashes. शिव called it violent. वृकासुर said it would prove शिव's love. शिव agreed and named him भस्मासुर, the ash demon.
[firm] Then पार्वती, शिव's wife, came down the slope. भस्मासुर stared. He could not have her while her husband lived. He raised his hand toward शिव's head.
[curious] शिव ran. The boon would not spare even him. He ran in all ten directions, but the demon was always right behind. At last he reached वैकुंठ, the home of विष्णु.
[calm] विष्णु smiled. He already knew. शिव asked for protection. विष्णु vanished, and a woman stood in his place.
[calm] She was मोहिनी. भस्मासुर forgot everything, even his boon. She said, "Come. Dance with me." He copied every step and every turn.
[firm] Then she placed her hand on her head. So did he.
[curious] मोहिनी laughed as she whirled around a pile of ash.

=== Indra Cut an Unborn Child | Indra Tried to Kill an Unborn Child. It Backfired. ===

[curious] इंद्र, king of the gods, once entered a mother's womb to kill her unborn son. That son was growing up to be his killer. इंद्र stopped him with one trick. It did not work the way he planned.
[calm] दिति was the mother of the demons. विष्णु had killed her sons हिरण्याक्ष and हिरण्यकशिपु. The gods under इंद्र had killed many more. She asked her husband, कश्यप, for a son who would kill इंद्र.
[calm] कश्यप agreed, with strict rules. The child would grow in her womb for a hundred years. All that time she must live as an ascetic and never lie down without washing her feet. If she failed, her son would befriend इंद्र instead.
[firm] दिति followed every rule. इंद्र watched for years, waiting for one mistake. She made none. Then only three days remained.
[curious] That evening, दिति was tired. She fell asleep without washing her feet. इंद्र leapt in. He entered her womb and cut the child into seven pieces with his वज्र, his thunderbolt.
[firm] The pieces were alive. They wailed. इंद्र shouted, "Ma ruda," which means, "Don't cry." They only grew louder. He cut each piece into seven more. They still would not die.
[calm] दिति woke and इंद्र vanished. She had lost. She gave birth to forty-nine children. They were no threat to इंद्र. They became his companions, as कश्यप had said. Because of his cry, they were called the मरुत, the storm gods.
[curious] इंद्र had cut a killer into forty-nine friends.

=== Kadru Cheated Vinata | Kadru Cheated Her Sister Vinata, and Garuda Paid for It ===

[curious] कद्रू cheated her sister विनता in a bet over a horse's tail. The loser would become a slave. And it was the loser's son who had to pay.
[calm] कद्रू, mother of a thousand serpents, and her sister विनता were wives of the sage कश्यप. विनता's two eggs would not hatch. Impatient, she cracked one open. Inside was half-formed अरुण. He cursed her to become her sister's slave, unless the other egg hatched on its own.
[calm] कद्रू boasted about her son वासुकि, who had been विष्णु's churning rope for the ocean. From it rose a white horse, उच्चैःश्रवा. विनता said it was white to the tail. कद्रू said the tail was black. The loser would be the winner's slave.
[firm] कद्रू's sons told her the tail was white. She ordered them to coil around it so no white hair showed. Some refused, and she cursed them. At dawn the tail looked black. विनता admitted defeat and became a slave.
[calm] Five hundred years later, the second egg hatched. गरुड़, half bird and half man, demanded his mother's freedom. The serpents wanted अमृत, the nectar of immortality. गरुड़ broke through fire, spinning blades and guard snakes and brought it.
[firm] The serpents freed विनता. इंद्र swooped in and took the nectar back. A few drops fell on sharp grass. The serpents licked them and split their tongues. That is why snakes have forked tongues.
[curious] विष्णु made गरुड़ his mount. विनता smiled.

=== He Lost Everything | King Harishchandra Lost His Kingdom, His Wife and His Son ===

[curious] King हरिश्चंद्र lost his kingdom, his wife and his son. He lost them to keep one promise. And the hardest test came at a cremation ground.
[calm] हरिश्चंद्र, king of अयोध्या, had once broken a vow to the water god वरुण. He swore never again. One day a spirit made him insult the sage विश्वामित्र. To make amends, he promised the sage anything. विश्वामित्र took his throne, his wealth and अयोध्या.
[calm] The king left with his wife, तारामती, and son, रोहित. The sage demanded दक्षिणा, a payment for accepting charity. The family found no work in काशी. तारामती asked to be sold as a maid. हरिश्चंद्र refused until she threatened to die. It raised only half the payment.
[firm] विश्वामित्र told him to sell himself to a चांडाल, a man who burned the dead. हरिश्चंद्र agreed. He worked at the cremation ground.
[curious] One night, a woman came with her dead son and no money for the fee. हरिश्चंद्र said he could not help. Then he saw her face. She was तारामती. The boy was रोहित, bitten by a snake.
[firm] She begged him to break his word just once. He refused. He had given his word to his master, and the gods were watching. She grabbed an axe to end her life.
[calm] Then विश्वामित्र called her name. रोहित ran to her alive. The sage said he could not defeat the king, and gave back everything. The चांडाल was यम, the god of death. He said the king's name would stand for truth.

=== A King Played Vishnu | A King Dressed as Vishnu to Replace Krishna ===

[curious] King पौंड्रक dressed up as विष्णु to replace कृष्ण. His courtiers swore it worked. कृष्ण's army had a different reaction.
[calm] पौंड्रक ruled the eastern land of पुंड्र. He was a friend of जरासंध and an enemy of कृष्ण, the यादव king of द्वारावती. When कृष्ण killed जरासंध, पौंड्रक raged. His flatterers told him कृष्ण was a thief, and that पौंड्रक was the true विष्णु.
[calm] They said it again and again. पौंड्रक believed it. He renamed himself वासुदेव, a name of विष्णु, and sent कृष्ण a decree. Give up the title and the divine weapons, or fight.
[firm] कृष्ण smiled, then laughed. He said he would fight.
[curious] पौंड्रक marched on द्वारावती. He had painted his body blue and wore yellow cloth. He had strapped two wooden arms behind his own to look four-armed. He wore a fake कौस्तुभ gem, and a गरुड़ banner flew over his chariot.
[calm] कृष्ण's soldiers laughed. Some rolled on the ground. कृष्ण said gods create humans, but a human can never be a god. पौंड्रक was only a man behind a mask.
[firm] पौंड्रक snapped. He called कृष्ण the fraud and demanded his weapons. कृष्ण said, "Take them. Take them all." He hurled his conch, bow, mace and disc.
[calm] The disc cut off पौंड्रक's head. His wooden arms fell off. The rain washed the blue paint off his skin.
[curious] कृष्ण warned everyone to beware of the पौंड्रक of the Earth. One false god, he said, would plague people more than a hundred demons.

=== His Herd Left Him | An Elephant Called Out to Vishnu While a Crocodile Held His Leg ===

[curious] A crocodile clamped onto the leg of गजेंद्र, king of the elephants. His whole herd walked away. When विष्णु came, his first words were a name गजेंद्र did not expect.
[calm] Near the Ocean of Milk stood Mount त्रिकूट. Beside it was a lotus pond where elephants came to drink. Their leader was गजेंद्र, a king tusker devoted to विष्णु.
[firm] One day, गजेंद्र trumpeted in terror. A crocodile had seized his leg. Blood clouded the pond. The herd knew a crocodile is stronger in water. They watched helplessly, and then they left.
[calm] गजेंद्र was alone. He stopped fighting. He lifted a white lotus in his trunk and prayed to विष्णु. He said he could do nothing to save himself. Protect me, he said, or free me if my hour has come.
[curious] The morning turned dark. A blue shape spread across the sky, a blue finger holding the सुदर्शन disc. The disc flew down and cut off the crocodile's head.
[calm] विष्णु smiled. He asked, "Have you learnt your lesson, इंद्रद्युम्न?"
[firm] गजेंद्र froze. The name stirred a past life. He had been a king named इंद्रद्युम्न. When the sage अगस्त्य came to his court, he did not even open his eyes to greet him. The sage cursed him to be born as an elephant, to learn to surrender.
[calm] The crocodile, विष्णु said, was a गंधर्व named हूहू, who had insulted the sage देवल.
[curious] गजेंद्र bowed to the ground before विष्णु.
