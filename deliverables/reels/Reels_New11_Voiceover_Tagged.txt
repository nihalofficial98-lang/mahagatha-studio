[warm] Markandeya grew up and loved Shiva with all his heart. On his sixteenth birthday, Yama came to take his life. [fearful] The boy was scared. He hugged the Shivling and prayed. Yama threw his rope at the boy, and the rope fell around the Shivling too. [pause]
[angry] This made Shiva very angry. He came out of the Shivling and killed Yama. [pause]
[surprised] But now no one was left to take care of death. So the gods begged Shiva to bring Yama back. Shiva did. And he blessed Markandeya to stay sixteen forever. [pause]
[confident] Because of this story, Shiva is also called Kalantaka, the one who ended Yama. [pause]
