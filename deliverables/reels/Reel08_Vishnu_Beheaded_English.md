## Plain narration
Vishnu lay asleep with his chin on his bow. Termites were chewing the bowstring under his face, and every one of them was a god. How did Vishnu lose his head?
Vishnu, who protects the world, had fought one demon for sixteen thousand years. His mace flew back and smashed his chariot. The demon laughed. "A god blessed me. Only the one who knows my secret can kill me."
Vishnu went home, very tired, and fell asleep. The gods, led by Brahma, who made the world, needed him awake for a yagna, a fire prayer. Their own powers had failed against the demon. Brahma had an idea. If the bowstring snapped, the sound would shake the whole sky.
So the gods chewed. Thinner and thinner. Faster and faster.
Twang. The string whipped loose and cut off Vishnu's head. It rolled away. His eyes were still closed. "What have we done?"
Then Lakshmi, his wife, walked in. Her feet left red marks. "Forgive me," she said. The demon had prayed to her so hard that she gave him a gift. He could die only at the hands of a god with a horse's head.
"That's why!" the gods cried. Lakshmi said, "Get the head of a horse. Vishnu will be Hayagriva again, the horse-headed god."
Soon horse-headed Vishnu neighed, and the demon fell silent. The gods had cut off Vishnu's head by mistake, trying to wake him. And that mistake gave them the one god who could kill the demon.
