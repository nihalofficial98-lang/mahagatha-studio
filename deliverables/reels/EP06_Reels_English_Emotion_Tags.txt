EP06 REELS - ENGLISH - EMOTION-TAGGED VOICEOVER SCRIPT (mild emotions only)
Read the [tag] as a light colour, not acting. Do not read the tags aloud. Keep a steady, natural pace; short pauses at full stops.

==================================================
REEL 1 - The God Who Has Almost No Temples
==================================================
[calm, curious]
The god who created the universe has almost no temples. It's because of one lie, and a flower that was forced to tell it.

[measured, storytelling]
This story comes from the Puranas. Brahma and Vishnu were arguing over who was greater. Suddenly, a pillar of fire appeared between them. It had no top and no bottom.
The gods set a challenge. Vishnu would find its root. Brahma would find its peak. Whoever returned first would win.

[steady, slightly brisk]
Vishnu became a boar and dug down. Brahma became a swan and flew up. Neither found an end.

[calm, honest]
Vishnu came back and admitted he had failed.

[slightly tense, quiet]
Brahma had failed too. But a Ketaki flower drifted down from above, and he saw his chance. He ordered it to testify that he had reached the peak. It hesitated. Then it agreed.

[low, serious]
Brahma claimed victory. The flower backed him. Then the pillar split open, and Shiva appeared. The pillar was Shiva himself.

[firm, controlled]
"Liar," he said.

[quiet, final]
From that day on, no one would worship Brahma. And no one would ever offer the Ketaki flower to Shiva.

==================================================
REEL 2 - Shiva Cursed Brahma. Well, Not Really.
==================================================
[light, knowing]
Shiva cursed Brahma so that no one would worship him. Well, not really.

[calm, explaining]
We've all heard it that way. But in the story, Shiva says it himself:

[firm, even]
"This is not a curse. This is the law of nature."

[measured, storytelling]
Here is what happened. Brahma lied to win a contest against Vishnu. A Ketaki flower was his false witness. Then Shiva rose from a pillar of fire and exposed the lie. He said something strange.

[firm, controlled]
"Hari accepted his defeat. But you were so afraid of losing that you cheated."

[calm, reflective]
Then he explained the result. You denied the truth, so the world will deny you. It was not a punishment born of anger. It was a consequence.

[soft, respectful]
That is why Brahma's temples are so rare today. But it doesn't erase what he did. He created the universe. His mistake wasn't evil. It was the fear of losing.

==================================================
REEL 3 - Brahma Lied. The Flower Paid For It.
==================================================
[calm, curious]
Brahma lied to win. But the one punished along with him was a flower.

[measured, storytelling]
It started with a race to find the end of an endless pillar of fire. Brahma flew up as a swan and found nothing. Then a Ketaki flower drifted down from above. It said it had slipped from the pillar's heights.

[slower, slightly tense]
Brahma heard only two words: the heights.

[quiet, persuasive]
He asked the flower to testify that he had reached the peak.

[uneasy, hesitant]
The flower said, "You want me to lie?"

[smooth, quietly pressing]
Brahma said, "I created you. I'm asking for one favour."

[quiet, heavy]
After a long silence, it agreed.

[serious, firm]
Then Shiva appeared and exposed everything. To Brahma he said, no one will worship you. To the flower he said,

[firm, with a touch of sadness]
"You were dear to me. But you gave false testimony. No one will offer you to me again."

[calm, thoughtful]
Brahma lost his worship. The Ketaki lost its place at Shiva's feet. So who was more guilty?

[slower, open]
The one who asked for the lie, or the one who agreed to it?
