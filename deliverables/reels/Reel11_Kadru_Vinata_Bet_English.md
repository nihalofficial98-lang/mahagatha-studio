## Plain narration
At dawn, two sisters stood behind a white horse. Its tail looked black, because snakes were wrapped around it. One sister had just lost her freedom to that trick. How did her son Garuda free his mother Vinata from Kadru?
Kadru and Vinata were sisters, wives of the sage Kashyapa. Kadru had a thousand snake sons. Vinata wanted two sons stronger than all of them. One day they argued about a white horse, Uchchaishrava. Vinata said it was white, every inch. Kadru said its tail was black. They made a bet. The loser would be the winner's slave.
Kadru's sons warned her. "The tail is white. Every hair." Kadru said, "White can be black." She told them to wrap themselves around the tail.
Vinata saw black. She became a slave. She had cracked open one of her two eggs too early. Out came half-formed Aruna. "You will be your sister's slave," he said. Only the second egg could end the curse.
Five hundred years later, it cracked. Garuda, half bird and half man, came out. "Free my mother," he said. The snakes agreed, for a price. "Bring us Amrut, the drink of immortality."
Fire ringed the Amrut. Blades spun at the door. Garuda laughed. "Venom is guarding Amrut?" He put out the fire with river water, shrank to slip through the blades, and trampled the snakes.
He put the pot down before the snakes. They let Vinata go. Garuda freed his mother by winning the one prize they asked for.

## Source lines (story 40)
1. Bet: Kadru "spawned one thousand serpents"; Vinata asked "only two sons who would surpass Kadru's thousand"; the sisters argue about the tail of Uchchaishrava (white vs black) and "The loser would have to become the winner's slave."
2. Aruna: Vinata "smashed one of them prematurely"; the "half-formed" embryo curses her: "enslaved by your sister, Kadru ... only if you permit the other egg to hatch on its own." Vasuki as churning rope; Uchchaishrava rose from the ocean ("Pristine white from head to tail").
3. Cheat: sons say "It's white. All white."; Kadru: "coil around that horse's tail ... not a single white hair is visible"; "many refused ... cursed them"; Vinata "accepted defeat".
4. "Five hundred years later" the egg hatched, Garuda "half-bird and half-human"; Amrut guarded by "ring of fire", "killer blades", "Two serpents"; "pleased that he had not partaken any of it".
5. Indra "swooped in. Clutched the vessel"; Amrut "spilled on the grass"; "tongue of every serpent would hereafter be forked"; "Sri Hari has made me his mount." "Vinata was smiling." The source says the snakes licked "those sharp blades"; the script says sharp grass because the Amrut fell on grass.
6. Cut: Garuda's deal with Indra, Kadru's final taunt, the shedding of skin. Name "Kadru, daughter of Daksha" dropped for length.
