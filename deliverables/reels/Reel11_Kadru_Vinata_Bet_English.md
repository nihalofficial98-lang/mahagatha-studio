## Plain narration
Kadru cheated her sister Vinata in a bet over a horse's tail. The loser became a slave. How did her son Garuda free her?
Kadru, mother of a thousand serpents, and her sister Vinata were wives of the sage Kashyapa. Vinata's two eggs would not hatch. Impatient, she cracked one open. Inside was half-formed Aruna. He cursed her to become her sister's slave, unless the other egg hatched on its own.
The ocean had been churned, and a white horse, Uchchaishrava, rose from it. Vinata said it was white to the tail. Kadru said the tail was black. The loser would be the winner's slave.
Kadru's sons told her the tail was white. She ordered them to coil around it so no white hair showed. Some refused, and she cursed them. At dawn the tail looked black. Vinata admitted defeat and became a slave.
Five hundred years later, the second egg hatched. Garuda, half bird and half man, demanded his mother's freedom. The serpents set a price: amrita, the nectar of immortality. Garuda broke through fire, spinning blades and guard snakes and brought it.
The serpents freed Vinata. Indra swooped in and took the nectar back. A few drops fell on sharp grass. The serpents licked them and split their tongues. That is why snakes have forked tongues.
That is how Garuda freed his mother. He brought the amrita through fire, blades and guard snakes. Vishnu made him his mount, and Vinata smiled.

## Source lines (story 40)
1. Bet: Kadru "spawned one thousand serpents"; Vinata asked "only two sons who would surpass Kadru's thousand"; the sisters argue about the tail of Uchchaishrava (white vs black) and "The loser would have to become the winner's slave."
2. Aruna: Vinata "smashed one of them prematurely"; the "half-formed" embryo curses her: "enslaved by your sister, Kadru ... only if you permit the other egg to hatch on its own." Vasuki as churning rope; Uchchaishrava rose from the ocean ("Pristine white from head to tail").
3. Cheat: sons say "It's white. All white."; Kadru: "coil around that horse's tail ... not a single white hair is visible"; "many refused ... cursed them"; Vinata "accepted defeat".
4. "Five hundred years later" the egg hatched, Garuda "half-bird and half-human"; amrita guarded by "ring of fire", "killer blades", "Two serpents"; "pleased that he had not partaken any of it".
5. Indra "swooped in. Clutched the vessel"; amrita "spilled on the grass"; "tongue of every serpent would hereafter be forked"; "Sri Hari has made me his mount." "Vinata was smiling." The source says the snakes licked "those sharp blades"; the script says sharp grass because the amrita fell on grass.
6. Cut: Garuda's deal with Indra, Kadru's final taunt, the shedding of skin. Name "Kadru, daughter of Daksha" dropped for length.
7. Title is the owner's final question title. Answer: serpents' condition "Bring us amrita and we shall set your mother free"; Garuda crosses "ring of fire", "killer blades" and "Two serpents"; "The serpents let Vinata go." The Vasuki churning-rope detail was cut for length.
