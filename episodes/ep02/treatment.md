# Episode 02: Director's Treatment (for your approval)

*Written as three people at once: the 2D vector artist (what gets drawn), the director (what each beat should make you feel), and the cinematographer (where the camera is and how the light behaves).*

## 1. The idea in one line
Brahma keeps building and the universe keeps pushing back. **The episode is a colour story: it starts in warm gold and green, turns cold silver-white for the Kumaras, burns red-black for Rudra, then sinks into storm indigo and a wilting lotus.** You should be able to tell where we are in the story from the colour alone, even with the sound off.

## 3. New things I will draw (all new for this episode)

**Characters**
- The four **Kumaras**: four child sages, same height and age but each with a distinct colour, pose and hairstyle (white cloth, rudraksha beads, closed eyes, floating in lotus pose).
- **Rudra / Shiva (fierce form):** ash-blue skin, red burning eyes, matted hair, third eye, trident, a body of fire at birth.
- **Eleven Rudras:** a repeating "assembly line" shot of identical figures, each a different shade.
- **Saptarishi:** seven sages in a line, each with a different staff and beard.
- **Dharma the bull:** four legs, and he visibly loses one leg per yuga (Satya → Treta → Dwapar → Kali) as a single continuous animation.
- **Narada:** young, with a veena and a tilak, expressive face (defiance, then hurt).
- **Agni, Kamadeva, Daksha, Vishwakarma:** quick cameo figures, one per birth.
- **Brahma upgrades:** sad, wilting-lotus version, and a furious version with red-lit faces.

**Worlds and props**
- The eight mountains (Sumeru, Kailash, Himalaya, Malay) rising in parallax; seven island-continents as an animated map; the three worlds plus Vaikuntha as a vertical "tower of realms"; four yuga pillars in white, red, yellow and black; a lightning-struck cosmos for the curse; a wilting lotus.

## 4. Camera language (the cinematographer's part)

| Technique | Where it is used and why |
|---|---|
| **Crane / rising shot** | World-building. The camera lifts to reveal mountains and continents, which sells scale. |
| **Symmetrical wide, locked-off** | The Kumaras. A still, balanced frame makes them feel unmovable. |
| **Low-angle hero shot** | Rudra's birth. Looking up at him makes him enormous and dangerous. |
| **Dolly zoom (vertigo effect)** | Brahma realising the Rudras are immortal. The background stretches while he stays still. |
| **Whip pan and repetition** | The 11 Rudras. Quick pans along a line of identical figures. |
| **Rack focus** (blurring foreground vs background) | Used on the curses, so attention jumps between Brahma and Narada. |
| **Slow motion + silence** | "बेटे ने… पिता को श्राप दे दिया." The frame nearly freezes. |
| **Dutch tilt (slanted camera)** | Brahma's fury; the world feels off-balance. |
| **Push-in on the eyes** | Rudra's "मेरा नाम क्या है?" and Narada's refusal. |
| **Pull-back to tiny figure** | Brahma alone after the curse, small under a huge dark sky. |
| **Handheld shake** | Only at the moment of the curse and the cosmos trembling. |

**Lighting rules:** one clear key light per scene (sun, fire, moon or a glowing character), always a rim light on the edge facing it, soft contact shadows under feet so characters sit *in* the scene, and volumetric light rays through haze.

## 6. Engine upgrades I would build for this episode
These make it look like a produced film, not a slideshow:
- **Depth-of-field blur** and **rack focus**, **film grain**, soft **vignette**, **letterbox bars** (cinematic 2.39:1) on the dramatic beats.
- **Colour grading** per sequence (the palette above applied as a consistent look).
- **Volumetric light shafts**, **anamorphic lens flares**, **bloom** on fire and eyes.
- **Walk cycles** (for the Kumaras and Narada leaving) and a **bull rig** for Dharma.
- **Motion trails and speed lines**, **ash and ember particles**, **lightning**.
- **Animated title cards and name captions** (for example the Saptarishi names appearing in light).
- **Depth in every scene:** 5–6 parallax layers (far sky, mid mountains, ground, characters, foreground silhouettes, floating particles).
- **Target edit pace:** about **120–140 cuts** (roughly one every 4–5 seconds), slower on awe beats and faster on conflict beats.
