# Episode 05 — Scene brief ("भगवान के द्वारपालों को श्राप, और दक्ष का नारद को श्राप")

Built against the owner's real voice (`voice_timeline.md`, 528.2 s, 16 beats). 180 shots, average 2.93 s, shortest 1.08 s (the "रुक जाओ" shout, deliberate), longest 5.04 s is not used; the longest shots are 4.84 s (Sindhu lost in the sea), 4.64 s (Adishakti teaching Brahma) and 4.6 s (the kumara's question). Machine-readable twin: `shot_plan.json` (ids b01_1 ... b16_7; every `at` is `{t, word}` copied from the timeline). Pause stingers used: 0.70 s (B01 to B02), 0.52 s (B02 to B03), 0.64 s (Narada's name), 0.50 s (B10 silence), 0.52 s (Daksha's roar), 0.54 s (Shiva reveal).

Conventions: heroes/travellers face right, blockers and threats face left (Jaya/Vijaya at the gate, Daksha at Narada). Vishnu sits right of frame facing left whenever the guards plead. Thunder is only ever in the BACKGROUND layer (thunder_flash, lightning images at par 0.2), never a red crack. Angry coded figures (Daksha) use brow -0.9 and a red aura. Bottom 18% kept clear of faces and text. Camera types never repeat in consecutive shots (push / pull / pan / tilt / locked / dutch alternate).

Cast (only what exists): coded Vishnu, vishnu_rest, Brahma, Daksha, Narada, Manu, Brahmi (as Shatarupa), Adishakti, Shiva; library cut-outs `lib_kumara`, `lib_jaya`, `lib_vijaya`, `lib_prasuti`, `lib_sati`, `lib_aditi`, `lib_kashyapa`, `lib_chandra`, `lib_surya`, `lib_indra`, `lib_diti`, `lib_danu`, `lib_hiranyaksha`, `lib_hiranyakashipu`, `lib_ravana`, `lib_uttanapada`, `lib_urvashi`, `lib_kubera`, `lib_vasuki`, `lib_villager_man`, `lib_villager_woman`, `lib_asura_soldier`, `lib_deva_generic`. The four Kumaras are the one `lib_kumara` cut-out repeated four times at 230-330 px height beside 580-900 px guards, so they read as child-sized. Crowds (5,000 / 1,000 sons, 60 daughters) are two or three rows of small cut-outs plus a text number.
