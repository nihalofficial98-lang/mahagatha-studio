## 1. Sonic concept
This is a story of a pillar of fire with no beginning and no end, and a lie told in front of it. The score is built on one idea: **a cluster that never resolves** (C-Db-G, phrygian/malkauns, choir + sub + fire) that sits under the pillar whenever it is on screen, and the whole episode is the question of whether Brahma's bright, rising four-note motif can survive next to it. Brahma is warm organ in Bhairav and gets brassier and more brittle as his ego grows. Vishnu is a bansuri in Yaman, humble and open. Shiva is a low horn and damaru in Malkauns and arrives only in B11. The Ketaki flower is a falling celesta/santoor petal in Marwa that later plays upward (the lie). Sound is sparse under the voice (music about 14 dB under the speech, thin 300 Hz-3.5 kHz), blooms only in the gaps, and goes fully quiet before the three reveals (the pillar cracks, "झूठे!", the trident). Tempo family: 66-72 dread heartbeat, 96-120 quarrel and flight, 48-60 for Shiva's verdict. Palette is earthy and procedural: tanpura, organ, choir, cello, horn, flute, celesta, taiko/timpani, fire, wind, wings, thunder. No melody is borrowed from any film or song.

## 6. Mix notes
- Stems are rendered at "gap level" and ducked by the renderer under the real voice envelope: bed -14, motif -14, ostinato -15, pulse -15, ambience -16, riser -15, hit/sfx -12 dB (60 ms look-ahead, 400 ms release; pauses shorter than 0.3 s count as speech). Hits placed in short gaps have their own `duck_db` (-6 to -10).
- Voice pocket: beds, ostinati and motifs have a static -4 dB cut at 300 Hz-3.5 kHz; registers are low (octave 2-3) except bells, celesta and flute.
- Gap-level targets: beds -28 dB RMS, ambience -30 dB RMS, pulse -17 dB peak, motif/ostinato -22 dB peak, hits -8 dB peak (impacts at the reveals -3 to -5). Final preview: `make_preview.sh`, loudnorm I=-16, TP=-1.5. Per-stem trims `BED= MOTIF= PULSE= AMB= SFX=` (dB) are env variables of the script.
- The ONLY cues that touch a key word: S18 (on "चटक") and the Om S19 (after "ओम"; ducked -8). Every other impact lands in a gap or after the word; the three big silences Z09, Z10, Z12 precede them.
- Danger spots to check by ear after the first render: Z10 (the 0.58 s silence before "झूठे") and H13 at 337.93 (is the gap really 0.5 s), S24 at 377.44 (the 0.46 s gap), the voice-free flight window in B7 (212.2-218.0), and the short-gap hits H04/H05/H12/S16/H19/H20.

## 7. Asset and feature requests
- The synthesiser is procedural (additive + filtered noise + convolution reverb). Organ, choir and horn are stylised, not sampled; for a final master a sampled engine or the owner's packs would improve them.
- Needs a 3 s end hold on the video (`PAD=3` in `mix.sh`) for the logo tail; the voice ends at 478.4.
- Earshot (clone at /home/user/leonxlnx/earshot) is a UI/motion-SFX and measurement tool, not a score generator; its venv is used only for numpy/scipy/soundfile. `earshot measure` can be used on the preview for LUFS/peak/clipping.
- Schema extensions beyond `library/SOUND_CAPABILITIES.md` that the renderer understands: `voicing` (degree lists per bed segment), `subdiv`, `repeat`/`repeat_gap`, `rate`, `reverse`, `degree`, `amb_depth_db`, `no_mask`, `from.a` (anchor type).
