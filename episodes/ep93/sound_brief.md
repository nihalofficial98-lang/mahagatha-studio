# EP93 Sound Brief: "Before Everything" (1 min test cut, 68.4 s voice)

## 1. Sonic concept
The piece goes from emptiness to the first sound. It begins in almost nothing: a tanpura on Sa and a cosmic hum, in dark Malkauns colour. The audience should feel that the void is vast and listening. At "कुछ भी नहीं" the music is cut completely (silence is the loudest thing there). Then a slow riser builds toward one word, "हिला।", where a single soft sub boom and a glass-like santoor figure (the FIRST STIRRING motif) arrive like a heartbeat. Beat 2 carries the heartbeat and a hint of dread (flat-2nd cluster) for "ख़तरा". At Parabrahman the score changes world: the heartbeat vanishes, the mode turns to Yaman, and a choir/organ open fifth with a gong sounds (formless, eternal, no pulse). Desire is a rising bansuri line. The vibration brings back the stirring motif, faster and higher, with a timpani roll and a riser, which is then cut dead one second before Om. Om arrives as a huge sub boom, gong, conch and a low choir bloom (Sa, Pa, Sa), and then stays as a warm drone under the last line, ending on the series sound (tanpura, conch, low boom). Tempo family is slow (48-60 bpm, no fast section), key D throughout. Voice always comes first.

## 6. Mix notes
- Music sits about 18-22 dB under the voice during lines (intensity 2-4 means 18-22 dB under, intensity 5-6 means about 14-18 dB under only inside gaps). Sidechain duck 4-6 dB under every spoken word (see duck_db per cue), release over about 0.3 s.
- Voice pocket (250 Hz-4 kHz) stays empty under speech: beds are sub/low (below 250 Hz) or airy high (above 4 kHz); santoor and flute motifs sit sparse, soft, an octave up, and mostly on held notes.
- Full-range swells only in the gaps: 28.90 (short), 34.40-35.10, 50.62-51.24, 61.32-62.02, and most importantly 62.62-63.72 (Om).
- Silences (c04, c07, c15, c18, c26, c34) must be real: reverb tails of the preceding cue must not run into them, except the Om tail in c35.
- Keep the final output limited around -1 dBFS true peak; the Om boom is allowed to be about 6 dB above the loudest earlier cue.
- Last word "थी।" (67.56-68.30) is not covered by the logo; the logo starts at 68.30.
