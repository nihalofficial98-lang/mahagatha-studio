# EP04 Sound Brief: "Shiva cuts Brahma's head, and Narada's Maya" (stories 6 + 7, 491.9 s voice)

All times are from `episodes/ep04/voice_timeline.md`. Cue ids refer to `episodes/ep04/sound_cues.json`.

## 1. Sonic concept
Part 1 is a story of a curse that bends a body, a flight that spans the whole animal world, one decisive blow, and a long walk to forgiveness. It is scored in **E**, dark and heavy: contrabass, french horn, trombone, cello, tremolo strings, taiko and dhol, damaru, with Todi/Phrygian/Malkauns colour for dread and wrath, Bhairavi for Brahmi's tenderness and fear, Bhairav and Yaman for Kapali and Kashi. The main theme, SHRAAP (E-F-G-E, a chromatic turn that cannot escape itself), is the curse and is heard in a different form in every stage of the story. The mid-point is a chase at 132-168 bpm (the fastest music of the episode), then the **fifth-head cut at 168.88 s is the biggest hit**, followed by almost 8 s of stillness (one long low note and wind). The emotional core is Brahmi's speech to Manu: one solo cello on a tanpura, climbing one step with each "उन्हें X देने से" line, and breaking open into a full swell on **"मनु।" (315.20 s)**, the biggest emotional swell. Part 2 turns to **G** and changes the world: bright pizzicato / glockenspiel / veena for Narada's pride, luminous Yaman for Vishnu, a shimmering marwa santoor and detuned pad for Maya, a long, warm, dreamlike river life, and a storm and flood (150 bpm timpani roll, wave hits) that is cut dead by Vaikuntha's stillness. It ends serene and wise (Eternal open fifth, moksha), then a question and a darker teaser (SHRAAP returns) and the series logo. Tempo families: slow (48-72), build (84-108), chase (132-168), playful (100-104), storm (150).

Because the owner's voice is tight (only 10 pauses of 0.35 s or more) all swells happen under or between words, kept low and in the bass or air, not in the voice pocket. The owner's voice gets quiet when it is tagged hushed, so music stays supportive and mostly at intensity 2-5.

## 6. Mix notes
- Voice first. Music 18-22 dB under speech at intensity 2-4; at 5-7 about 14-18 dB under and only with a 4-6 dB sidechain duck (duck_db per cue). Release about 0.3 s.
- Voice pocket (250 Hz-4 kHz) must stay empty under speech: beds below 250 Hz or above 4 kHz; the cello in Brahmi's speech is the one solo that lives in the pocket and must be very soft (-22 dB) and sparse, filling only the gaps between words; santoor, celesta, glock and flute an octave up.
- The owner's voice is quiet when tagged hushed: do not set the music's intensity by the guide numbers alone; keep the whole bed 2 dB lower in B10 and B16 and check against the voice rendering.
- Biggest hit: c110 at 168.88 (no pause there). Low-passed boom and a sparse high crack, with a 0.25 s voice-band dip only. It must be the loudest event of the episode: roughly 6 dB above the flood (389.24) and Kashi (270.98).
- Biggest swell: c202 at 315.20 to 316.02, peak 9 (about 2 dB below the 168.88 hit). Strings, choir, horn pedal, soft sub, no cymbal. Duck the bed only a little (-4 dB) so the swell can be felt but "मनु।" stays clear.
- Silence cues that are real pauses (>= 0.35 s): c136 (209.16-209.64), c179 (278.70-279.32), c186 (290.36-291.00), c248 (377.48-378.08), c275 (414.48-415.12). All other 'silence' cues are music-only drop-outs between words, and must not cut the voice. Clear reverb tails there.
- The stillness 169.98-177.82 is deliberate: one low note, wind, nothing else; anything above intensity 3 spoils it.
- Peak limiter about -1 dBFS true peak.
- Errata for the cue file: in `sound_cues.json` the c278 note says "0.4 s of voice-free air after 'चुभी'" (this is not measured: the real gap after 'चुभी।' is unknown), and the requests line listing true pauses also names c219 and c268, which are SFX cues, not silences. Treat both as wording only; the anchors are correct.
