The gods cut off Vishnu's head. They did it on purpose.
Every one of them had turned into a termite. How did Vishnu lose his head?
Vishnu protects the world. He had fought one demon for 16,000 years and could not win.
His mace flew back and smashed his own chariot. The demon told him that only someone who knew
his secret could kill him. Vishnu went home very tired and fell asleep with his chin resting
on his bow. The gods, led by Brahma, who made the world, needed Vishnu awake for a fire prayer
called a yajna. Their own powers had failed against the demon. Brahma had an idea. If the bow string
snapped, the sound would shake the whole sky. So the gods turned into termites and chewed the
string. Thinner and thinner. Faster and faster. Then it snapped and the string whipped loose
and cut off Vishnu's head. It rolled away. His eyes were still closed. The gods froze. They knew
they had made a terrible mistake. Then Lakshmi, his wife, walked in. Her feet left red marks on
the floor. She asked them to forgive her. The demon had prayed to her for so long
that she had given him a gift. He could only be killed by a god with a horse's head.
That was why the demon could not be beaten. Lakshmi told the gods to bring the head
of a horse and put it on Vishnu. He became Hayagriva, the horse-headed god. Soon Vishnu
nade and the demon fell silent. The gods cut off Vishnu's head by mistake,
only to wake him. And that mistake gave them the one god who could kill the demon.
