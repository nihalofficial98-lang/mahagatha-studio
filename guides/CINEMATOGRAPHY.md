# Cinematography
- 1280×720 design space, rendered at 1.5× (1080p) or 3× (4K). Safe area for text: 80 px margins; subtitles bottom band (gradient strip, serif, spoken word bright/upcoming dim).
- Lighting: warm key from front-left, cool rim light from the opposite side (figures get an 8-direction silhouette outline).
- Depth: 3 parallax layers (sky 0.1, mid clouds 0.3, floor 0.9, figures 1.0). Camera moves ≤ 10% zoom, ≤ 40 px drift, rotation ≤ 0.4°.
- Post: bloom, grade soft-light per mood, film grain, vignette.
- Palette per world: celestial lavender/pink; storm/rage crimson+black; night navy; amber for earth/dharma; cosmos violet.
