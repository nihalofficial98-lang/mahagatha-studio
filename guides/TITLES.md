# Title rules (from the owner's reference channel screenshots)
Examples: "Why Didn't Parshuram Kill Ravan?", "How Huge Was Varaha Avatar?", "What Happened to Ashwatthama After the Mahabharat War?", "Garud: The Bird Who Defeated Indra", "VEERBHADRA - The Warrior Born From the Wrath of Mahadev", "Kumbhkaran Wounded Hanuman ji. Then This Happened", "Ravan Destroyed Ayodhya and Killed the Ikshvaku King".
Thumbnail / first-frame text: 2-4 big words ("Why Not Kill Bali?", "Ashwatthama Still Alive", "Bird who challenged the gods").

## Rules
1. **At least one character name, early in the title.** The viewer must know who it is about at a glance.
2. **Say plainly what the story is about.** A direct question or a striking fact; no vague riddles ("Seven Lives of Love or Three of Hate?" is out).
3. Formats that work: `Why Didn't/Did X ...?` | `How/What ...?` | `X: The <role> Who <did>` | `X did Y. Then This Happened` | a bold fact statement.
4. Full title 6-12 words. First-frame text 2-4 words, same subject, bigger and punchier.
5. Truthful: the video must pay off exactly what the title asks.
6. Reels: the first-frame text is the title of the reel; give 3 options ranked, each as `first-frame text` + `full title`.

## Opposite-notion rule (owner)
Every title and thumbnail text states the opposite of what people believe about the character (see guides/REEL_STYLE.md, "Opposite-notion hook"). Example: "The Only Demon Shiva Ran From".
